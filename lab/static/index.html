COVERAGE + LOCAL REFINEMENT
Define “just enough”

Coverage = fraction of interior grid within half a track spacing of a reading. Interior: x 0.4–7.6 m, depth 0.2–2.7 m. This measures proximity to tracks, not temperature accuracy or guaranteed feature detection. At most four slower local cross/box passes.

CONTROLLER-OWNED EXPLORATION

Descend vertically at the selected x. A sustained measured temperature change triggers a slow upward retrace with a lag/detection margin check, then a bounded local investigation. Visited locations are remembered for the entire run. Each local investigation or hold lasts at most 8 s, then move to a nearby unvisited target. The mission continues until Stop.

No sweep or automatic area-coverage route. Each local region stays within 0.45 m horizontally and 0.45 m above / 0.10 m below the detection point. No total mission time or travel limit. Local bursts and holds: maximum 8 s each.

Quiet water: profile down to 2.7 m, then move toward unvisited water. Local probes use two short adjoining legs; target selection penalizes known-water transit and sharp turns. No sweep route. If the bounded tank has no useful unvisited targets left, show No unvisited target (this does not establish temperature accuracy). Press Stop to end exploration.

Mapping notes & equations

Left: the simulated field, known only to the experiment. Right: a local estimate using position and delayed probe readings. Grey means unsampled; faded colours mean weak support. Arrows point toward warmer water; they do not show water currents.

Support is a heuristic based on nearby sample geometry and fit residual, not a confidence interval. A straight track cannot determine a full 2D gradient. Dashed lines mark the mid-temperature isotherm, not every possible front. Coordinates are exact in this simulation; real navigation error is not modelled.

Do we solve a PDE?

No. Temperature is a number at each location. A gradient is a pair of slopes: temperature change per metre horizontally and vertically. We fit T ≈ a + b·x + c·depth to nearby measurements; (b, c) is the estimated gradient. The simulator uses a chosen static temperature formula. Solving a heat-transfer PDE would be a separate, later modelling problem.

How to read this experiment

Table: mean across the three independent repeats. Coverage counts occupied bins out of 36 between 0.10 and 1.00 m; errors use only bins occupied by every strategy. Transition errors use ±0.15 m around the unique mid-temperature boundary. Boundary error uses the first measured crossing in travel order; “—” means absent or undefined. Early completion, censoring and faults appear below. No recovery or repeated cycles are hidden in the allowance.

Actual lag and noise come from Setup; the vertical controller assumes τ = 2 s. Positions are exact, water is static, probe/depth offset is zero, and thruster mixing is absent. Energy in the CSV is a toy propulsion proxy. Raw observations, reference, commands, learned bands, seeds and settings are retained for audit.
