Define “just enough”

Coverage = fraction of interior grid within half a track spacing of a reading. Interior: x 0.4–7.6 m, depth 0.2–2.7 m. This measures proximity to tracks, not temperature accuracy or guaranteed feature detection. At most four slower local cross/box passes.

CONTROLLER-OWNED EXPLORATION

Descend vertically at the selected x. A sustained measured gradient triggers a bounded partial upward inspection and local investigation, limited to 8 s. Nearby unvisited-water sampling then lasts at most 10 s total, across all waypoint changes.

Return to the original descent line and the depth where the detour began. Resume descent; pass another 0.25 m before allowing a new investigation. Unvisited targets stay within 0.45 m horizontally and 0.45 m above / 0.10 m below the detection point. Visited memory is retained.

At 2.7 m, ascend directly to the surface and finish. Quiet water goes straight down and up. No sweeps or unlimited frontier loop. If checkpoint return fails within 15 s, attempt ascent from the current location; a stalled ascent reports recovery failure after 60 s. Stop remains available throughout.

Mapping notes & equations

Left: the simulated field, known only to the experiment. Right: a local estimate using position and delayed probe readings. Grey means unsampled; faded colours mean weak support. Arrows point toward warmer water; they do not show water currents.

Support is a heuristic based on nearby sample geometry and fit residual, not a confidence interval. A straight track cannot determine a full 2D gradient. Dashed lines mark the mid-temperature isotherm, not every possible front. Coordinates can include the configured observation errors in controller exploration. Browser free swim uses exact simulated coordinates.

Do we solve a PDE?

No. Temperature is a number at each location. A gradient is a pair of slopes: temperature change per metre horizontally and vertically. We fit T ≈ a + b·x + c·depth to nearby measurements; (b, c) is the estimated gradient. The simulator uses a chosen static temperature formula. Solving a heat-transfer PDE would be a separate, later modelling problem.

Evaluation definitions and limits

Raw readings use estimated probe depth. Forward reconstruction uses the filtered-regressor support depth, not the present vehicle depth. Boundary depth is the strongest supported gradient peak in the declared 0.20–0.80 m search range. A unique layer is required for boundary error. Coverage counts all 32 required bins; full-profile RMSE is absent unless every bin is observed. Covered and transition errors are conditional. Paired intervals resample whole scenarios and require both detectors to succeed; failure counts accompany them.

The sensor model is first order with configurable mismatch, noise, bias, quantization, position errors, packet loss and moving layers. A speed floor can make the requested lag tolerance unattainable. Neither the estimator nor the speed rule has been validated in real water. Energy is a toy proxy. Physical probe calibration, an independent reference and a vertical rig are the next milestones.
